English

Last updated: 22 November 2026. Applies to: Bade Mahjong for Android, version 1.0.0 (build 34), package com.akotech.bademahjong. Publisher: AkoTech.

In short. Bade Mahjong keeps your game progress and settings on your own device. It has no account or sign-in, no developer-operated server, no ads, and no analytics or crash-reporting code of our own. The first 40 levels are free; one optional, one-time purchase through Google Play unlocks all 3,000 levels. Google Play handles the payment, we never see your card or payment details, and nothing is sent to us. Because of that purchase, the app contains Google's Play Billing library, and the app asks Android for network access (see section 2). Between levels the app may ask Google Play to show Google's own rating card; Google draws it and we receive nothing from it. You can contact us at the email address below.

1. Who we are and how to contact us

Bade Mahjong is published by AkoTech. Privacy questions and requests: akn.kaplanoglu@gmail.com. This is the only public contact channel. This page does not state a postal address or country of establishment.

2. What the app stores on your device

The game saves the following in the app's private Android storage, as files that only the app can read. The app does not add its own encryption to them.

WhatWhy
Game progress: finished levels, your balloons and their colours, best altitudes, stars, and an unfinished levelSo you can continue where you left off.
Settings: sound, volume, ambient sound, vibration, reduced motion, text size, look, sound set, difficulty and languageTo remember your choices.

None of this is sent to us. The app does not ask for or store your name, email address, birth date, phone number, contacts, location, photos, files, microphone or camera data, advertising ID, or any account identifier.

Android permissions: the installed app lists these permissions. com.android.vending.BILLING lets the app ask Google Play about the full-game purchase. android.permission.INTERNET and android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE are added by Google's Play Billing library, which uses them for its own communication with Google; Bade Mahjong's own code makes no network requests and there is no server of ours for it to talk to. The remaining one is an internal permission that Android's own support library adds to protect the app's broadcast receivers. Google's rating card needs no permission of its own: it is a window that the Google Play app shows. None of them gives the app access to your contacts, location, photos, files, microphone, camera or advertising ID, and you are not asked to approve any of them: Android grants them when you install the app.

Copying the trial summary. Settings › Trial summary has a button that copies the summary described above to your phone's clipboard. Nothing leaves the app unless you press it and then paste the text somewhere yourself.

3. Ads and purchases

This version shows no ads. The first 40 levels are free. One optional, one-time in-app purchase through Google Play, shown as "Full game", unlocks all 3,000 levels. It is not a subscription: nothing renews and nothing is charged again.

How a purchase works. When you choose to buy, Google Play shows its own purchase screen and processes the payment under Google's terms and privacy policy. The app only receives a confirmation from Google Play, on your device, that the purchase is complete. We never see your card or payment details, your name or your email address through this, and we run no server that receives purchase information. The app confirms ("acknowledges") the purchase to Google Play and keeps the single "unlocked" flag described in section 2. The flag is not tied to any account of ours.

Restoring. Whenever the app starts, and when you open the purchase screen, it asks Google Play in the background for the price and whether your Google account already owns the full game, so you can unlock it again on a new phone or after clearing the app's data. "Restore purchase" asks the same question on request. This needs an internet connection; playing the levels does not, and nothing is shown if Google Play does not answer.

Rating card. After you finish a level, at level 15 or later and never on the way to the purchase screen, the app may ask Google Play to show its own rating card. Google Play decides whether the card appears and draws it; you can rate or close it without leaving the game. The app does not see your rating or any text you write, and nothing is sent to us. The app asks at most three times in total and at least 30 days apart, and keeps only that count and the date of the last request on your device (section 2). Google's privacy policy applies to the card.

Google's Play Billing library. This is Google's code, built into the app. Besides talking to the Google Play app, it may send its own technical and usage information to Google, as described in the Google Privacy Policy. We do not receive or control that information.

Refunds and payment questions are handled by Google Play under its refund policy. We cannot see or change your purchases.

If this changes. If a later version adds ads, subscriptions or any other data handling, this policy and the store's data disclosures will be updated before that version is released.

4. Backups and deleting your data

The app does not turn off Android's backup feature. Depending on your device, Android version and settings, your app data may be included in a backup held by Google or your device maker and may be restored on a new device. We do not control or have access to those backups.

Because the game data lives on your device, you delete it yourself: open Android Settings → Apps → Bade Mahjong → Storage → Clear data, or uninstall the app. This removes your progress, settings, the trial summary and the "unlocked" flag. If you bought the full game, use "Restore purchase" afterwards to unlock it again at no extra charge; the purchase itself belongs to your Google account and is not removed by clearing data or uninstalling. Any backup copy is managed in your Google account or device-maker backup settings. There is no in-app delete button in this version.

5. Age

Bade Mahjong is a general puzzle game made with adult players in mind, and it is not directed at children. It does not ask anyone, including children, to enter personal information. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has sent us personal information, for example by email, contact us at the email address above so we can review the request.

6. This website

This page is hosted on GitHub Pages. When you open it, GitHub may process technical data such as your IP address, browser and device details and request logs under the GitHub General Privacy Statement. This is separate from the app. We do not add analytics, cookies, scripts or external fonts to this page, and we do not run any tracking on it.

7. If you email us

If you write to us, we receive your email address, the name shown on your account, your message and anything else you include. We use it only to read and answer your message and to handle your request. The email is held in the publisher's email account, which is provided by Google (see the Google Privacy Policy). We keep it for as long as needed to handle the request and keep a record of it, and then delete it when it is no longer needed. We have not set a fixed number of days. Please do not send sensitive personal information; you never need to send us your game save.

8. Your choices and rights

We do not hold your game data, so there is nothing for us to look up or delete about it; you control it on your device as described above. For emails you have sent us, you can ask us to tell you what we hold or to delete it. Depending on where you live, local law may give you additional rights; this page does not state which law applies to the publisher. Purchase records are held by Google under your Google account; ask Google for access to them or to delete them.

9. Security

Your data stays in the app's private storage, protected by Android's app sandbox. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, and we make no guarantee of absolute security.

10. Third-party services

Google Play (installing and updating the app, backup, the optional purchase through Google Play Billing, and the rating card) and GitHub (hosting this page) are separate services with their own privacy terms: Google Privacy Policy and GitHub General Privacy Statement.

11. Changes to this policy

When we change this policy we will update the date at the top of this page. Changes that add data collection, ads or purchases will be published before the version that introduces them is released.

3. Reklam ve satın alma

Bu sürüm reklam göstermez. İlk 40 bölüm ücretsizdir. Google Play üzerinden yapılan, isteğe bağlı ve tek seferlik bir uygulama içi satın alma ("Tam sürüm" adıyla görünür) 3.000 bölümün hepsini açar. Bu bir abonelik değildir: hiçbir şey yenilenmez ve bir daha ücret alınmaz.

Satın alma nasıl işler. Satın almayı seçtiğinizde Google Play kendi satın alma ekranını gösterir ve ödemeyi Google'ın koşulları ve gizlilik politikası kapsamında yürütür. Uygulama yalnız, satın almanın tamamlandığına dair Google Play'den cihazınızda bir onay alır. Bu yoldan kart veya ödeme bilgilerinizi, adınızı ya da e-posta adresinizi görmeyiz ve satın alma bilgisini alan bir sunucu işletmeyiz. Uygulama satın almayı Google Play'e onaylar ("acknowledge") ve bölüm 2'de anlatılan tek "açık" bayrağını saklar. Bayrak bize ait hiçbir hesaba bağlı değildir.

Geri yükleme. Uygulama her açıldığında ve satın alma ekranını açtığınızda, arka planda Google Play'e fiyatı ve Google hesabınızın tam oyuna zaten sahip olup olmadığını sorar; böylece yeni bir telefonda ya da uygulama verilerini temizledikten sonra tekrar açabilirsiniz. "Satın alımı geri yükle" aynı soruyu isteğiniz üzerine sorar. Bunun için internet bağlantısı gerekir; bölümleri oynamak için gerekmez, Google Play yanıt vermezse ekranda bir şey gösterilmez.

Puanlama kartı. Bir bölümü bitirdikten sonra, 15. bölümden itibaren ve satın alma ekranına giderken asla olmamak üzere, uygulama Google Play'den kendi puanlama kartını göstermesini isteyebilir. Kartın görünüp görünmeyeceğine Google Play karar verir ve kartı o çizer; oyundan çıkmadan puan verebilir ya da kapatabilirsiniz. Uygulama verdiğiniz puanı ya da yazdığınız metni görmez, bize hiçbir şey gönderilmez. Uygulama toplamda en çok üç kez ve en az 30 gün arayla ister; cihazınızda yalnız bu sayıyı ve son isteğin tarihini tutar (bölüm 2). Kart için Google Gizlilik Politikası geçerlidir.

Google'ın Play Billing kütüphanesi. Bu, uygulamanın içine gömülü Google kodudur. Google Play uygulamasıyla konuşmanın yanında, Google Gizlilik Politikası'nda anlatıldığı gibi kendi teknik ve kullanım bilgilerini Google'a gönderebilir. Bu bilgileri biz almayız ve denetlemeyiz.

İade ve ödeme soruları Google Play'in iade politikası kapsamında Google Play tarafından yürütülür. Satın almalarınızı göremez ya da değiştiremeyiz.

Bu değişirse. Sonraki bir sürüm reklam, abonelik veya başka bir veri işleme eklerse, bu politika ve mağazadaki veri beyanları o sürüm yayımlanmadan önce güncellenir.

4. Yedekler ve verilerinizin silinmesi

Uygulama Android'in yedekleme özelliğini kapatmaz. Cihazınıza, Android sürümünüze ve ayarlarınıza bağlı olarak uygulama verileriniz Google'ın veya cihaz üreticinizin tuttuğu bir yedeğe dahil edilebilir ve yeni bir cihazda geri yüklenebilir. Bu yedekleri biz denetlemeyiz ve onlara erişimimiz yoktur.

Oyun verileri cihazınızda durduğu için onları siz silersiniz: Android Ayarlar → Uygulamalar → Bade Mahjong → Depolama → Verileri temizle yolunu izleyin ya da uygulamayı kaldırın. Bu, ilerlemenizi, ayarlarınızı, deneme özetini ve "açık" bayrağını siler. Tam oyunu satın aldıysanız, sonrasında "Satın alımı geri yükle" ile ek ücret ödemeden yeniden açabilirsiniz; satın almanın kendisi Google hesabınıza aittir ve veri temizlemek ya da uygulamayı kaldırmakla silinmez. Yedek kopyalar Google hesabınızın veya cihaz üreticinizin yedekleme ayarlarından yönetilir. Bu sürümde uygulama içinde bir silme düğmesi yoktur.
