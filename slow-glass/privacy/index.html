English

Last updated: 15 November 2026. Applies to: Slow Glass for Android, version 1.0.1 (build 2021), package com.akotech.slowglass. Publisher: AkoTech.

In short. This version of Slow Glass keeps your game progress and settings on your own device. It has no account or sign-in, no developer-operated server, and no analytics or crash-reporting code of its own. Ads and purchases are switched off in this version. You can contact us at the email address below.

1. Who we are and how to contact us

Slow Glass is published by AkoTech. Privacy questions and requests: akn.kaplanoglu@gmail.com. This is the only public contact channel. This page does not state a postal address or country of establishment.

2. What the app stores on your device

The game saves the following on your device, in the app's private Android storage (Android “SharedPreferences”). The app does not add its own encryption to it.

WhatWhy
Game progress: current level, move count, stage, reward piece, collected pieces, “expansion seen” flagSo you can continue where you left off.
Settings: sound muted, colour-blind modeTo remember your choices.
Language choiceTo remember the language you selected (or “follow the device”).
A copy of a save that could not be readKept only so a damaged save is not silently lost.

None of this is sent to us. In this version the app does not ask for or store your name, email address, birth date, phone number, contacts, location, photos, microphone or camera data, advertising ID, or any account identifier.

Android permissions: the installed app declares INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE, com.android.vending.BILLING, WAKE_LOCK and FOREGROUND_SERVICE. These are added by the libraries the app uses (audio, Google Play Billing, and Google Mobile Ads). None of them asks you for runtime access to location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files. The advertising-ID and Privacy Sandbox ad permissions are removed from the app.

3. Ads and purchases: currently off

In this version there are no ads, no purchases, no ad-consent form and no age question. The app is configured so that ads and purchases stay disabled unless a build is deliberately configured otherwise, and the shipped configuration leaves them disabled.

For transparency: the app package contains Google Mobile Ads (including Google's User Messaging Platform) and Google Play Billing libraries, which are switched off by configuration. Based on our source review, the app does not start them in this version. We have not captured the app's network traffic, so this page does not promise that no Google or Android component ever communicates with a server (for example, Google Play itself when you install or update the app, or Android's normal system services).

If this changes. If a later version turns on ads, purchases, analytics or any other data collection, this policy and the store's data disclosures will be updated before that version is released. The current design for a future version is: no ads for users who are under 13, for users with an unknown age range, or for users who have not completed the consent step; purchases would be handled by Google Play, and a purchase receipt (store purchase token and order details, not your name) could be sent to a verification service run by the developer. This describes a design, not a commitment to release such a feature.

4. Backups and deleting your data

The app does not turn off Android's backup feature. Depending on your device, Android version and settings, your app data may be included in a backup held by Google or your device maker and may be restored on a new device. We do not control or have access to those backups.

Because the game data lives on your device, you delete it yourself: open Android Settings → Apps → Slow Glass → Storage → Clear data, or uninstall the app. This removes the local game progress, settings and language choice. Any backup copy is managed in your Google account or device-maker backup settings. There is no in-app delete button in this version.

5. Age

Slow Glass is intended for people aged 13 and over. This version does not contain an age check that enforces this (no age question is shown while ads are off). This version does not ask you to enter personal information into the game. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has sent us personal information, for example by email, contact us at the email address above so we can review the request.

6. This website

This page is hosted on GitHub Pages. When you open it, GitHub may process technical data such as your IP address, browser and device details and request logs under the GitHub General Privacy Statement. This is separate from the app. We do not add analytics, cookies, scripts or external fonts to this page, and we do not run any tracking on it.

7. If you email us

If you write to us, we receive your email address, the name shown on your account, your message and anything else you include. We use it only to read and answer your message and to handle your request. The email is held in the publisher's email account, which is provided by Google (see the Google Privacy Policy). We keep it for as long as needed to handle the request and keep a record of it, and then delete it when it is no longer needed. We have not set a fixed number of days. Please do not send sensitive personal information; you never need to send us your game save.

8. Your choices and rights

We do not hold your game data, so there is nothing for us to look up or delete about it; you control it on your device as described above. For emails you have sent us, you can ask us to tell you what we hold or to delete it. Depending on where you live, local law may give you additional rights; this page does not state which law applies to the publisher.

9. Security

Your data stays in the app's private storage, protected by Android's app sandbox. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, and we make no guarantee of absolute security.

10. Third-party services

Google Play (installing and updating the app, backup) and GitHub (hosting this page) are separate services with their own privacy terms: Google Privacy Policy and GitHub General Privacy Statement.

11. Changes to this policy

When we change this policy we will update the date at the top of this page. Changes that add data collection, ads or purchases will be published before the version that introduces them is released.

4. Yedekler ve verilerinizi silme

Uygulama Android'in yedekleme özelliğini kapatmaz. Cihazınıza, Android sürümünüze ve ayarlarınıza bağlı olarak uygulama verileriniz Google veya cihaz üreticisi tarafından tutulan bir yedeğe dahil edilebilir ve yeni bir cihazda geri yüklenebilir. Bu yedekleri biz denetlemeyiz ve bunlara erişimimiz yoktur.

Oyun verileri cihazınızda durduğu için onları kendiniz silersiniz: Android Ayarlar → Uygulamalar → Slow Glass → Depolama → Verileri temizle yolunu izleyin veya uygulamayı kaldırın. Bu işlem yerel oyun ilerlemesini, ayarları ve dil seçimini siler. Yedek kopyalar Google hesabınızdaki veya cihaz üreticisinin yedekleme ayarlarından yönetilir. Bu sürümde uygulama içinde bir silme düğmesi yoktur.
